Frequently Asked Questions

How do I place an order?

Email Elizabeth at thedutchyofmillbrook.contact@gmail.com with “CAKE” in the subject line — include the date you need the cake and the best number to reach you — or fill out the Custom Orders form. Because every cake is made to order, please reach out as early as you can.

What does a charm cake cost?

Charm cakes come in three sizes: 8″ (serves 12–16) is $650, 10″ (serves 22–24) is $850, and a half sheet (serves 50–60) is $2,500. Wedding, lattice, trellis and other custom cakes are bespoke — priced by size and the complexity of the design.

How do charm cakes work?

A charm cake is like an edible charm bracelet. Send Elizabeth a list of the people, pets, places and passions you’d love on the cake, in order of importance — there’s no set number; it depends on how detailed each one is, and she’ll fit on as many as she can. Let her know what you’d like for the inscription (for example, “Happy Birthday”), and for pets or anything unusual, send a photo so she can capture it just right.

What flavors and fillings can I choose?

The most popular cakes are white and chocolate, but Elizabeth can make almost anything — the only flavors she doesn’t do are funfetti and red velvet. The outside is always vanilla French buttercream. Between the layers, choose from chocolate ganache, chocolate hazelnut, homemade raspberry or cherry jam, passion fruit, or lemon curd.

How do I get my cake — pickup or delivery?

Elizabeth is in New York City every week, with cakes in tow. You can pick up from her apartment on the Upper West Side, collect in Millbrook on Saturdays or Sundays, or arrange delivery (the price varies with distance).

How do I store and serve my cake?

Cakes must be refrigerated. Take yours out of the fridge about an hour before serving so the French buttercream can soften back to room temperature — that’s when it tastes its best.

Do you make wedding cakes?

Yes — from single-tier celebration cakes to multi-tiered wedding cakes, all in her signature hand-painted style. Reach out with your date and vision and she’ll take it from there.

Can you accommodate allergies?

Please mention any allergies when you reach out. Everything is baked in a home kitchen that also handles nuts, dairy, eggs and wheat.

How far in advance should I order?

As early as possible. Charm cakes are made to order and dates fill up, so the more notice you can give, the better the chance Elizabeth can say yes to your date.
