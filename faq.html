Frequently Asked Questions

How do I place an order?

To place an order, fill out our Order Form or email Elizabeth at thedutchyofmillbrook.contact@gmail.com with “CAKE” in the subject line. Please include the date you need your cake and the best phone number to reach you.

All cakes are made to order, so we recommend reaching out as early as possible. Availability depends on Elizabeth’s baking schedule, and submitting an inquiry does not guarantee your requested date.

Pricing is based on the size and complexity of each cake and will be provided once the details of your order have been discussed.

How far in advance should I order?

As early as possible. Charm cakes are made to order and dates fill up, so the more notice you can give, the better the chance Elizabeth can say yes to your date.

How do charm cakes work?

Think of a charm cake as an edible charm bracelet. Each cake is decorated with hand-painted details that tell a personal story.

To get started, send Elizabeth a list of the people, pets, places, hobbies, and other meaningful details you’d like included, listed in order of importance. There is no set number of charms per cake; the final number depends on the size of the cake and the complexity of each design.

Please also include your preferred inscription (for example, “Happy Birthday”) and reference photos for pets or anything particularly specific.

What flavors and fillings can I choose?

Our most popular cake flavors are white and chocolate, but Elizabeth can accommodate most flavor requests, with the exception of funfetti and red velvet.

All cakes are finished with vanilla French buttercream. Filling options include chocolate ganache, chocolate hazelnut, homemade raspberry or cherry jam, passion fruit, and lemon curd.

How do I receive my cake?

Pickup is available from Elizabeth’s apartment on the Upper West Side in New York City or in Millbrook, New York, on Saturdays and Sundays.

Elizabeth travels to New York City weekly with cakes, so pickup arrangements will be confirmed when your order is placed.

Delivery may also be arranged depending on location and availability. Delivery fees vary by distance.

Can I make changes or cancel my order?

We will do our best to accommodate changes to your order, but changes cannot be guaranteed and may be subject to additional charges. All change requests must be submitted by email.

Cancellations must also be made by email. Refunds are considered based on the timing of the cancellation and the status of the order. Orders canceled close to the scheduled pickup or delivery date may not be eligible for a refund.

How do I store and serve my cake?

All cakes must be refrigerated until shortly before serving.

Remove your cake from the refrigerator approximately one hour before serving to allow the French buttercream to soften. Larger cakes may need additional time. Cakes are best enjoyed when the buttercream has reached room temperature.

Avoid leaving your cake in direct sunlight or a warm environment, as buttercream can soften and lose its shape.

Do you make wedding cakes?

Yes! Elizabeth creates wedding cakes ranging from single-tier designs to elaborate multi-tiered cakes, all featuring her signature hand-painted details.

Please reach out with your wedding date, approximate guest count, and any ideas or inspiration you have in mind. Wedding cakes are priced individually based on size and design.

Have another question?

Please email Elizabeth at thedutchyofmillbrook.contact@gmail.com with any additional questions.
