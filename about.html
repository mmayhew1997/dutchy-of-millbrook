About

The Dutchy of Millbrook

For thirty years, Elizabeth Mayhew styled the pages of American homes — an editor at House Beautiful, Real Simple, and Woman’s Day, and a longtime contributor to The Today Show. But she decorated her first cake at age eight, and never really stopped.

When the world went quiet during the pandemic, she began baking for her isolating neighbors in Millbrook, New York. What started as comfort became a calling — and a name borrowed from The Cazalet Chronicles, with a “t” added as a wink to Dutchess County.

Her signature is the charm cake: a custom portrait in buttercream. She doesn’t sketch. She starts with a scalloped frame — “they’re like smiles linked together” — and doodles directly onto the cake, the way she once styled a room or a magazine cover.

“I have a sixth sense for where things belong. Decorating a cake is a lot like decorating a room.”
